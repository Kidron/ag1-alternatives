Things to know

  • The maker says to take it 30–45 minutes after eating a little, not on an empty stomach.
  • Possible gas or bloating in the first 1–2 weeks; start with half a scoop.
  • Contains flaxseed and a little cayenne.
  • Contains ginkgo, green tea extract, astragalus and other herbs. Check with a doctor if you take medications (for example, ginkgo and blood thinners).
  • Taste reviews are mixed ("earthy"). Version 22.0 added apple flavor and monk fruit.
  • No sugar alcohols.

Other Vibrant Health products

  • Green Vibrance Capsules: same formula, 16 capsules per serving, ~$3.40/serving (worse value than the powder).

Bottom line: Green Vibrance is the closest affordable one-scoop swap, with more fiber, more probiotics and full ingredient disclosure, but it doesn't have AG1's specific strains or full multivitamin. Try the $16 7-serving pouch at half a scoop first.

What the research says

Fiber and diverticular disease: the guidance from major health sources.

NIDDK (National Institutes of Health)

A high-fiber diet with less red meat may lower the risk of diverticular disease. The target is about 28 g of fiber a day on a 2,000-calorie diet, increased gradually.

Read source

American Gastroenterological Association guideline

Suggests a fiber-rich diet or a fiber supplement after diverticulitis (very low quality evidence, no set dose). Suggests against probiotics after uncomplicated diverticulitis.

Read source

AGA 2021 clinical practice update

Evidence is insufficient for probiotics to prevent recurrence, and a fiber supplement doesn't replace a good diet.

Read source
Takeaway: AG1's 2 g of fiber is small next to a 25–30 g daily target. Any benefit people notice may come from the daily routine, the prebiotic inulin, the probiotics, the minerals, or simply drinking extra water, and the research can't separate these.

Morning routine tip

Keep the habit, swap what's in the glass.

  1. Take it first thing. Mix your fiber into a full glass of water in the morning, before eating, the same way many people take AG1.
  2. Start small. Begin with a low dose and build up over a week or two to avoid bloating.
  3. Drink water. Keep drinking water through the day when adding fiber.
  4. Space out medications. Fiber can slow absorption of some medications, so keep them about 2 hours apart.
Cheapest test: psyllium husk you may already have. Mix 1 tsp into 8–10 oz of water first thing in the morning and drink it right away, building to 1 tbsp over 1–2 weeks. Keep medications 2 hours apart, and try it for 3–4 weeks before judging.
